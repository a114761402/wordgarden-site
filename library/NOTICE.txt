Word Garden default word-book library, generated by scripts/build-library.py.
Parts of this library are AI-written; each such part is labelled below.

English books (lib-en-*.json): which words belong to which exam (CET-4, CET-6, kaoyan, IELTS, TOEFL,
GRE) and their frequency order follow public exam word lists, as tagged in ECDICT (MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2025 Linwei, https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT , commit 82c9872576b23118d7c42e920c11beb77f510ae2;
only the tags and frequency fields are used). These are not official word lists and the app is not
affiliated with the organisations behind these exams. The Chinese meanings, British IPA, synonyms,
antonyms and Chinese root/affix notes were written by AI (Claude, Anthropic, 2026-10), checked for
format and spot-checked; they may contain mistakes. They are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Dutch books (lib-nl-freq-*.json): Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Meanings, part of speech, de/het and pronunciation: English Wiktionary contributors,
  https://en.wiktionary.org/ , extracted by kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract),
  file kaikki.org-dictionary-Dutch.jsonl.gz dated 2026-10-03.
- Word order: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (content CC BY-SA 4.0), nl_50k.txt from
  OpenSubtitles 2018 (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).
Modifications: inflected forms are credited to their lemma (was -> zijn, mensen -> mens);
archaic, dated, rare and dialectal senses are omitted; at most two parts of speech and
four senses are kept; homographs note the verb they also inflect.
These derived Dutch files are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

Dutch through Chinese books (lib-nlzh-freq-*.json): same words, order, parts of speech and
pronunciation as the Dutch books above. Chinese meanings were translated by AI
(Claude, Anthropic, 2026-10) from the English Wiktionary senses, using Chinese Wiktionary
(https://zh.wiktionary.org/ , kaikki.org extract 2026-10-02, CC BY-SA 4.0, OpenCC simplified)
as a hint, with ambiguous words reviewed and corrected. Shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Chinese through Dutch books (lib-zh-hsk-*.json): words, levels, frequency order and pinyin from
complete-hsk-vocabulary by Yanis Zafirópulos (MIT, https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary,
commit 7ac65bf1a6387d35f1ade478906172a19311c7f9; HSK 3.0 lists from elkmovie/hsk30). Dutch meanings
were translated by AI (Claude, Anthropic, 2026-10) from the CC-CEDICT English senses
(https://cc-cedict.org/, CC BY-SA 4.0) in that file, and are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Chinese through English books (lib-zhen-hsk-*.json): same HSK 3.0 words, order and source as above;
English meanings are CC-CEDICT senses (CC BY-SA 4.0), shortened to at most four. For words with several
readings, the everyday reading and its senses were chosen by AI and reviewed (the source lists readings
alphabetically). Shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Dutch through Spanish books (lib-nles-freq-*.json): same words, order, parts of speech and
pronunciation as the Dutch books above. Spanish meanings were translated by AI (Claude, Anthropic,
2026-10) from the English Wiktionary senses, with ambiguous words reviewed. Shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Swedish (sv-en dictionary and lib-sven-freq-*.json wordbooks): English Wiktionary
contributors, https://en.wiktionary.org/ , via kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract),
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Swedish/ , extracted 2026-10-03.
Source file: kaikki-swedish.jsonl.gz; its SHA-256 is pinned in scripts/build-library.py
and recorded in the dictionary manifest. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Swedish wordbook order: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/sv/sv_50k.txt
(OpenSubtitles 2018, content CC BY-SA 4.0); SHA-256 pinned in scripts/build-library.py.
Modifications: selected and shortened senses; obsolete/rare/dialectal senses excluded;
frequency of inflected forms credited to lemmas (är -> vara); en/ett and IPA retained;
lookup lemmas plus inflected forms distributed in 128 shards and a headword-only spelling index.
The adapted Swedish data is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
Chinese meanings for lib-svzh-freq-*.json, when supplied, are translated and organised
with automated tools from the English senses: 借助自动化工具翻译整理，可能有疏漏。
They are also shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. No Chinese frequency books are generated
without a complete supplied Chinese translation. Starter glosses and examples are original;
Swedish starter IPA was checked against this Wiktionary extract (CC BY-SA 4.0).
