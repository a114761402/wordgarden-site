Word Garden lookup dictionaries (public/dictionaries), rebuilt on 2026-10-11 by
public/dictionaries/build-dictionaries.py. No ECDICT text and no FreeDict data is included.

English to Chinese (en-zh): about 30,000 words: the exam wordbook words and other common words. The Chinese meanings and the
British IPA were written by AI (Claude, Anthropic, 2026-10) and checked for format and spot-checked;
they may contain mistakes. Which words are in which exam, and the frequency order, follow public word lists and are not
official lists. These entries are shared under
Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Dutch to English (nl-en): English Wiktionary contributors, https://en.wiktionary.org/ , extracted by
kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract; file kaikki.org-dictionary-Dutch.jsonl.gz). CC BY-SA 4.0.
Modifications: Dutch lemmas and the inflected forms that point to them; archaic, dated, rare and
dialectal senses omitted; at most two parts of speech and four senses per lemma; organised into 128
lazy public shards. These derived files are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

The shard files are public, so anyone can download and reuse them under those licenses.
Personal wordbooks are not public.

Swedish (sv-en dictionary and lib-sven-freq-*.json wordbooks): English Wiktionary
contributors, https://en.wiktionary.org/ , via kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen, Wiktextract),
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Swedish/ , extracted 2026-10-03.
Source file: kaikki-swedish.jsonl.gz; its SHA-256 is pinned in scripts/build-library.py
and recorded in the dictionary manifest. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Swedish wordbook order: FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave,
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords/blob/master/content/2018/sv/sv_50k.txt
(OpenSubtitles 2018, content CC BY-SA 4.0); SHA-256 pinned in scripts/build-library.py.
Modifications: selected and shortened senses; obsolete/rare/dialectal senses excluded;
frequency of inflected forms credited to lemmas (är -> vara); en/ett and IPA retained;
lookup lemmas plus inflected forms distributed in 128 shards and a headword-only spelling index.
The adapted Swedish data is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
Chinese meanings for lib-svzh-freq-*.json, when supplied, are translated and organised
with automated tools from the English senses: 借助自动化工具翻译整理，可能有疏漏。
They are also shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. No Chinese frequency books are generated
without a complete supplied Chinese translation. Starter glosses and examples are original;
Swedish starter IPA was checked against this Wiktionary extract (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Dutch to Spanish (nl-es), Dutch to Chinese (nl-zh) and Swedish to Chinese (sv-zh), 2026-11-19: the
meanings of the nl-en and sv-en entries above, translated into Spanish or Chinese by AI (Claude, Anthropic,
2026-11) for learners with a Spanish or Chinese interface, and checked for format and spot-checked; they may
contain mistakes. The 10,000 words of the matching library wordbooks and the inflected forms are not repeated
in them. Derived from English Wiktionary (see above) and shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
